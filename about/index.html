Before product

Before product, there was a review committee. At Federal Medical Centre Abeokuta, I was project manager for the Research and Ethics Committee — moving research proposals through a fixed review path: from the submitting researcher, to the professor and second readers assigned to it, through to a decision to stamp or reject.

The committee chairman told me the work suited me, and advised me to move into project management. That advice led directly to a Coursera course in project management, and from there into product.
